Professional Services & Support

While all HibbittsDesign.org open-source tools are freely available for anyone to use and benefit from, Paul also offers professional services for teams or organizations seeking personalized support. Drawing on experience from over 140 software projects spanning 25 years of professional consulting, these services help create better experiences with open content publishing, offered as availability permits alongside Paul's ongoing open-source work.

Whether you're looking to streamline your workflows or need custom development, Paul can help open publishers, educators, and creators leverage Markdown more effectively through Docsify, Grav CMS and online learning environments to increase efficiency and collaboration.

Optimize Workflows & UX

Streamline your Markdown publishing workflows and enhance the user experience of both authors and readers with Paul's personalized guidance. Based on availability, Paul can also provide informal workflow and UX assessments of online learning environments to ensure efficient workflows and a seamless user experience for both students and your team.

Priority Support Subscriptions

Gain prioritized access to Paul's Markdown publishing expertise for ongoing support, troubleshooting, and best practices with Docsify and the Grav CMS.

Tutorials and Workshops (online)

Equip your team or organization with the skills and knowledge needed to maximize the potential of Markdown publishing using Docsify or the Grav CMS.

Custom Configurations and Development

Tailored solutions that extend or optimize your use of Docsify-This or a Docsify Starter Kit for documentation-style static sites, or Grav Helios Course Hub and Open Reader for highly customizable, version-controlled websites – including open textbooks and other OER.

Interested in any of these services? Contact me and let's explore how we can enhance your digital projects together.
